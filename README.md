I am new to the development environment by many people's definitions. I have dabbled in development, tech, and systems off and on my whole life. This past year I focused in on understanding the world behind the world I love so much. I am still learning every day and every interaction, both professionally and personally.

Older versions of this said "I am not a developer." But I can't technically say that anymore. So I'll change it: I am not a programmer.

The me you see here came to be when I decided I wanted to run Claude Code on my Android phone. My [first bug](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/37723), [first PR](https://github.com/termux/termux-packages/pull/29074), the [fix that landed in Google's ChromeOS](https://chromium-review.googlesource.com/c/chromiumos/platform2/+/7881724). On the personal side I get a little more eclectic. I love the research and growth side of LLMs, the results and the why behind them. Some of my favorite hours are spent scrolling GitHub, reading JSON outputs, or running a script that A/B tests across thousands of seeds and multiple models just to see where the curve starts and stops. What surprises me? Where does the community consensus differ from my results?

## claude-code-android

When I was looking to get Claude Code on my Android phone I had trouble finding the depth or real info I wanted. It may have been out there, I just couldn't find it, so I decided to do mine my own way and keep working on it. Once I figured it out I launched the claude-code-android repo to help others who wanted to do the same. I had no plan/ goal. Just hey, let's make this a thing.

To my relative surprise it has gained some traction, and for that I am humbled. I do my best to keep the repo up to date and useful for those like me. I operate my agent I refer to as "Pilgrim" thanks to that repo. If that brought you here, I hope you liked it.
